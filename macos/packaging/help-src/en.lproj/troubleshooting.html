Troubleshooting

  • Scanning an access point with no internet: every lookup that needs a working resolver has a hard deadline, so the scan never sits waiting on something that can never arrive. Partial results stay on screen.
  • Missing MACs: the scan re-pings responders whose ARP entry was lost and re-reads the table. Entries outside the local subnet never have a MAC.
  • Rows seem missing: a text filter may be active — clear the search field. Rows with no open port yet gain their 80/8080 knock only after the harvests.
  • Export is disabled while a scan runs; stop it first.
