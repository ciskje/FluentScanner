Hostnames

  • Names come from three sources, best first: Bonjour (mDNS) harvest, NetBIOS node-status for Windows PCs, classic reverse DNS last. No failure is fatal — a missing name never blocks the rest.
  • Hosts seen only through harvests (mDNS/UPnP speakers that ignore ping and every discovery port) still get every clue: a post-harvest ARP re-read for MAC + vendor, reverse DNS and NetBIOS over the full row set, a short 80/8080 knock, and the UPnP label.
  • Quiet devices with no open port and no name service stay anonymous: open ports, a real MAC vendor and mDNS are what identify a device.
